Important Notice on Pricing Philosophy

At Royal Feast, we strictly avoid one-size-fits-all hidden pricing. The per-person rates listed above are illustrative starting guidelines intended to provide an approximate budgeting baseline for standard headcounts.

Your exact investment will be finalized in an itemized quotation that factors in: your confirmed guest count, specific choice of meats (prime mutton, baby lamb, beef, or poultry), live cooking stall logistics, venue location requirements, and prevailing market costs for premium ingredients.
