1. Introduction & Scope

At Royal Feast, we take the confidentiality of our clients, banquet hosts, and guests with paramount seriousness. This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Royal Feast collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information gathered from users of our website (royalfeast.com), booking forms, and inquiry channels.

2. Information We Collect

When inquiring about our catering services, requesting custom quotations, or booking an event, you may be asked to provide personal identifiers and event specifics, including:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number.
  • Event Parameters: Event category (wedding, corporate, birthday, etc.), anticipated date, expected guest count, and event venue location.
  • Culinary Preferences: Menu selections, cuisine preferences, dietary restrictions, and allergy considerations.
  • Technical Log Data: Browser type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically via server logs for security.

3. How We Use Collected Data

We process client information solely for legitimate banquet hospitality purposes:

  • Preparing detailed, itemized catering estimates and customized proposals.
  • Coordinating ingredient procurement, chef preparation schedules, and kitchen logistics.
  • Direct client communication regarding tasting sessions and banquet timelines.
  • Continually improving our website performance and user experience.

4. Form Transmissions & Email Integration

Our contact and custom quotation forms utilize frontend integration via EmailJS. When you submit a booking or quotation request, your provided inputs (name, phone, email, guest count, and message) are packaged and transmitted directly to our designated management mailbox: u382322@gmail.com.

We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease this correspondence to third-party marketing companies under any circumstances.

5. Data Protection & Security

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information. Transmission of inquiry data is protected using SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption protocols.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our site may utilize basic session cookies and performance telemetry to understand visitor navigation and maintain interface preferences (such as light/dark elements or form state retention). You may set your browser to refuse cookies or notify you when cookies are being sent.

7. Your Rights & Contact Inquiries

You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate event records, or request deletion of your contact records from our booking system.
